## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a dignified, warm, and inviting community sanctuary for India's plant-based movement. Eschewing clinical nutrition dashboards, corporate SaaS tropes, and confrontational activist motifs, the design language centers on communal warmth, hospitality, and shared living. 

The aesthetic synthesizes modern minimalism with organic tactile warmth:
- **Atmosphere:** Welcoming, grounded, and convivial. It mirrors physical Indian gathering spaces (addas)—rich with conversation, shared food, festive energy, and mutual respect.
- **Visual Stance:** Calm editorial layouts, generous natural negative space, soft earthen structural containers, and respectful, high-contrast typography.
- **Emotional Intent:** To instill feelings of belonging, friendship, cultural pride, and effortless discovery without dogmatism or moral friction.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural subcontinental landscapes—deep shade groves, fired clay, sun-warmed spices, and unbleached grain textures.

- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces:** The foundation relies on `#F9F6F0` (warm sand/linen), avoiding stark digital white. Mid-level containers, message bubbles, and cards utilize layered parchment `#F3EFE6` and crisp card surfaces `#FFFFFF` with warm stone boundaries `#E5DFD5`. Text leverages deep slate `#1E293B` to maintain effortless long-form legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Primary Accent (`#1B4332`):** Deep Forest Green commands primary actions, verified badges, active community tabs, and authoritative headline anchors. It conveys grounded growth and enduring vitality.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C25E3E`):** Sunbaked Terracotta powers interactive highlights, festive gathering callouts (such as the Ekatva festival), direct invitations, and emotional triggers (likes, cheers, RSVPs).
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D97706`):** Warm Ochre / Marigold serves as an energetic accent for badges, community milestones, festival dates, and micro-interactions.
- **Functional State Colors:**
  - Success: `#2D6A4F` (Earthen Pine)
  - Warning: `#B45309` (Amber)
  - Error: `#991B1B` (Deep Madder)
  - Info: `#0F766E` (Warm Teal)

## Typography

The platform utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly to establish a friendly, open, and contemporary social cadence. Its wide geometric counters and subtly rounded curves yield high readability at text scale while projecting hospitality and openness at display scale.

- **Headlines:** Set with firm weights (600–800) and compact negative letter spacing to create confident, grounded community anchoring.
- **Body Text:** Calibrated at generous line-heights (1.6 to 1.75) using `#1E293B` to sustain comfortable reading for personal stories, city guides, festival schedules, and discussion threads.
- **Labels & Metadata:** Styled with deliberate micro-letter-spacing and medium-to-bold weights for rapid skimming across event tags, timestamps, recipe markers, and location badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive fluid grid within a bounded maximum content width of `1200px`, maintaining intimate proportions and avoiding overly stretched widescreen feeds.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` outer page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Feed architecture follows an asymmetrical 3-column split (e.g., left navigation & city hub: 3 cols; central community timeline: 6 cols; right discovery & festival agenda: 3 cols).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. The secondary discovery rail collapses below or into structured horizontal carousels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Bottom navigation is persistent, touch targets maintain a minimum vertical height of `48px`, and horizontal carousels bleed to viewport edges with `1rem` padding.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Element gaps within cards leverage `space-sm` and `space-md`; gaps between timeline feed cards maintain a restful `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) to foster leisurely perusal without visual noise.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through tonal stratification and warm-tinted ambient shadows, rejecting stark monochromatic drop shadows or clinical corporate layering.

- **Base Canvas:** `#F9F6F0` (warm sand).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Resting Cards, Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting against `#F9F6F0`, anchored by an ultra-soft earthen outline (`1px solid #E5DFD5`) and a diffused warm shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns):** Elevated with a dual ambient shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Modals, Festival Action Sheets, Drawers):** High-level overlay resting at `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`, backed by an earthen backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.8)`).
- **Tonal Insets:** Input fields, comment regions, and secondary inner containers use `#F3EFE6` with subtle inner definition rather than stark outlines.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature to express natural, organic form factors and approachable hospitality:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Employed for small chips, count badges, tooltips, and contextual action menus.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Standard across community feed cards, discussion boxes, modals, media embeds, and profile banners.
- **Large Container Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Utilized for highlighted community feature panels, Ekatva festival promotional banners, and bottom navigation sheets.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for primary action buttons, status pills, filter tabs, and user avatars to maximize touch comfort and visual friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1B4332` with `#FFFFFF` text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `1.5rem`. On hover, shifts softly to `#2D6A4F` with subtle elevation.
- **Secondary / Festive Action:** Solid `#C25E3E` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for meetup RSVP, festival passes, and friend connections.
- **Outline / Gentle Button:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1.5px solid #E5DFD5` border and `#1E293B` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F3EFE6` with border shifting to `#1B4332`.
- **Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `#1B4332` text, padded `0.75rem 1rem`, ideal for secondary feed dismissal and subtle tab navigation.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Pill:** Pill-shaped, height `36px`, typography `label-md`.
- **Default / Unselected:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5DFD5`, text `#1E293B`.
- **Active / Selected:** Surface `#1B4332`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #1B4332`.
- **Festive / Highlight Tag:** Tinted terracotta background (`rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.12)`), text `#C25E3E`, bold font weight.

### Cards (Community Post & Event)
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5DFD5`, border-radius `1rem` (16px), internal padding `1.5rem`.
- **Header:** User avatar (`48px` circular), author name (`headline-sm`), city/neighborhood tag (`label-sm` in `#C25E3E`), and relative time.
- **Content:** Standard spacing `space-md` between text block and rich imagery. Images receive a mandatory inner radius of `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Footer Interaction Bar:** Generous action area with 44px touch targets for "Cheer", "Comment", "Share", and "Bookmark", separated by a hairline border (`#F3EFE6`).

### Lists & Social Activity Streams
- **Dividers:** Separated by low-contrast `#E5DFD5` hairlines or alternating tinted panels (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Activity Item:** Generous spacing (`1rem` vertical padding), avatar alignment on the left, primary metadata at top, inline action button on trailing edge.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Size:** `20px x 20px` with a minimum interactive hit zone of `44px x 44px`.
- **Unchecked:** Border `2px solid #E5DFD5`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checked:** Background `#1B4332` with clean white SVG checkmark. Radio buttons utilize a solid inner circle (`#1B4332`) with a `3px` white gap.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Minimum height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EFE6`, border `1.5px solid #E5DFD5`, border-radius `0.75rem` (12px), text `#1E293B`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#1B4332` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Helper & Validation Text:** Positioned `space-xs` below field; error state highlights border in `#991B1B` with accompanying helper icon.

### Specialized Community Components
- **Ekatva Festival Gathering Card:** Full-bleed terracotta-tinted backdrop (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFF7ED 0%, #FFEDD5 100%)`), deep terracotta borders (`#FDBA74`), featuring date badges, venue markers, and bold action triggers ("Join the Circle").
- **Local City Adda Indicator:** Small contextual tag with a soft ochre dot (`#D97706`), showing active real-time discussions across hubs (e.g., "Bengaluru Adda • 42 active now").
